Welcome to Beru

Beru is a progressive reggio inspired early years school in Jayanagar, Bengaluru. At Beru, we strive to nurture a curious, capable and resilient child filled with wonder and knowledge. We strongly believe in providing a supportive and enriching environment to connect parents, educators and the community as a whole.

Children are competent learners and they can do anything if given the right opportunity, right tools and the right environment. Beru provides just that and more - a school to ignite the natural curiosity of children, a place that values innovation and creativity, and a thriving community that comes together to celebrate learning.

Reggio Emilia

While our learning outcomes are based on Beru Early Years Framework (BEYF), our teaching approach is inspired by the Reggio Emilia philosophy which encourages children to learn through play, inquiry and their own exploration.

Holistic Programme

Developed in collaboration with a leading early childhood curriculum professor, our holistic programme prepares the children to meet the challenges of an increasingly connected world by helping them develop not only academic competencies but also social skills.

Empowering Children

Positively equipped with resilience, confidence, well-being, self-regulation and effective communication skills, children become empowered to thrive within a constantly evolving community.
